About Kosten

A personal finance tracker that helps you log income and expenses, organize them by category, and stay on top of a monthly budget — built as a semester-long Django project.

Problem

People lose track of where their money goes — there's no simple way to log spending, group it by category, and see whether they're staying within a budget.

Who uses it

Individuals managing their own personal finances.

What it does

Register and log in, record income and expenses, categorize transactions, set a monthly budget per category, view spending summaries, and (once the API phase is built) access the data through a REST API.
